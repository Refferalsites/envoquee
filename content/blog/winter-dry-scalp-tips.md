**Short answer:** shorter, warm (not hot) showers, a gentle shampoo, a little moisture and avoiding heaters blowing on your head can make a big difference.

## Why winter dries the scalp

Cold air holds less moisture, and gas heaters dry indoor air further. Very hot showers strip natural oils.

## Tips

1. Keep showers warm, not hot.
2. Use a mild shampoo and don't over-wash.
3. Light oiling a few hours before washing can help some people.
4. Wear a clean woolen cap and wash it often.
5. Pause derma rolling if your scalp is cracked, flaky or irritated, and restart once it is calm.

If flakes are thick, yellow or very itchy, it may be dandruff or dermatitis; see a doctor.
