**Short answer:** keep the scalp clean, massage it gently, avoid harsh heat, and keep any active steps such as derma rolling on a fixed, spaced-out schedule from your product directions.

## The weekly plan

**Wash days (2 to 4 times a week, more if you sweat a lot).** Use a mild shampoo and focus it on the scalp. Rinse thoroughly, especially with hard water.

**Daily 3-minute massage.** Use your fingertips, not nails, in small circles. It feels good and is a nice habit to keep.

**Oiling (optional).** If you love desi oiling, apply a few hours before washing rather than leaving it on a sweaty scalp all day.

**Derma roller day.** On a clean, dry scalp: antiseptic prep, gentle rolling, then serum. Follow the frequency printed in your kit. Never roll on irritated skin.

**Rest days.** Let the scalp recover. Skip heavy styling and tight hairstyles.

## Small things that add up

- Sleep on a clean pillowcase and change it often in summer.
- Avoid very hot showers and blow-drying on the highest heat.
- Eat a varied diet. Low iron and protein can show up in hair; a doctor can test this.
- Manage stress where you can. Shedding often follows stressful months.

## Keep it consistent

Hair grows slowly, so any routine needs months, not days, to judge. Take a photo in the same light once a month instead of checking the mirror daily.
