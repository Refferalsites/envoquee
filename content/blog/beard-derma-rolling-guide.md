**Short answer:** yes, many rollers are made for the face and beard area. The Envoquee roller is designed for both scalp and beard. Use it on clean skin, never right after shaving, and be patient.

## Why beards grow patchy

Beard density is mostly genetic, and it often keeps filling in through the twenties. Patchiness is normal and not a sign that anything is wrong.

## A careful beard routine

1. Wash your face and beard area and pat dry.
2. Spray the roller and skin area with antiseptic.
3. Roll gently in vertical, horizontal and diagonal strokes over the patchy areas. The cheek skin is thinner than the scalp, so use even less pressure.
4. Apply your serum and let it absorb.
5. Clean the roller and put it back in its case.

## Important don'ts

- **Don't roll right after shaving** or using a trimmer. Wait until the skin is fully calm.
- **Don't roll over pimples,** ingrown hairs or razor bumps.
- **Don't share your roller** with brothers or friends.
- **Don't roll before going out in the sun.** Do it at night.

## Realistic expectations

A routine can help your beard look neater and better cared for, but no product can change genetics. If you suddenly lose beard hair in round patches, see a dermatologist.
